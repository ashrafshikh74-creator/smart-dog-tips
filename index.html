3 Easy Tips That Actually Help

1

Tire the brain, not just the legs

Ten minutes of mental games can tire a dog out about as much as a long walk. Try one of these:

  • Hide a few treats under a towel and let your dog sniff them out.
  • Put treats in a muffin tin and cover each cup with a tennis ball.
  • Play the three-cup shuffle with a treat under one cup.
2

Redirect puppy biting

When your puppy nips, don't yank your hand away. That turns it into a game. Calmly offer a chew toy instead, and praise your puppy the moment they chew the toy.

3

Potty training is all about timing

Take your puppy outside right after they wake up, eat, play, or nap. Praise them the second they finish. Good timing does most of the work.
